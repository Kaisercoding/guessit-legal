2. Overview

Guess It is an estimation quiz that is mostly played in multiplayer mode. We process personal data for your account, for gameplay and for notifications. We limit processing to what is necessary to run the app. We do not sell data, show no ads in the app, use no tracking or analytics services and do not build user profiles for advertising.

3. Data we process

Account and sign-in data: If you register by email, we store your email address. With “Sign in with Apple” or Google sign-in, we receive a unique user identifier and – depending on what you choose with the provider – your email address (with Apple, possibly an anonymized relay address). Every account is assigned an internal identifier (UUID).

Guest account: If you play without registering (“Play right away”), we create an anonymous account without an email address. It is automatically given a name (e.g. “Guest-1042”).

Profile data: your chosen username and avatar (selected from predefined images), as well as the app language and your measurement system (metric or US). We take both from your device settings or from your choice in the app so that questions, units and notifications appear in your language. We do not collect your location.

Game data: ongoing and finished games, chosen categories, submitted guesses, round results, scores, experience points (XP), level, achievements, statistics and the times of your moves.

Friends data: your friends list as well as friend requests you sent and received.

Push notifications: a device token issued by Apple, used to notify you when it is your turn or when you receive a friend request.

Purchase and subscription status: whether and which variant of “Guess It Pro” (monthly, yearly, one-time) is active, and a transaction identifier. Payments are processed exclusively by Apple; payment data (credit card etc.) never reaches us.

Reports: If you report a username or a quiz question, we store the report, the reason you selected, the identifier of the reported account or question and your identifier as the reporting person – for review by moderation and to prevent abuse.

Submitted questions: If you suggest a quiz question via “Submit a question”, we store the question text, answer, unit, category, source, the app language and your identifier.

Support communication: If you contact us, we process your message and contact details to handle your request.

Technical log data: When our servers are accessed, technical data is generated (including IP address, time, requested function, error and status messages, app and device metadata). It is used for secure operation, troubleshooting and defense against attacks, and is stored only briefly.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing your account, profile and gameplay (including multiplayer, friends, leaderboards, achievements, statistics): performance of the user agreement, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Push notifications: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (iOS system prompt). You can withdraw it at any time in the iOS settings.
  • Handling purchases and managing Pro status: performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and compliance with tax and commercial law obligations, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Handling reports, moderation, abuse prevention, IT security and log data: legitimate interest in a secure, functional and rule-compliant service, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Reviewing submitted questions and adding them to the question catalog: legitimate interest in a well-maintained, growing question catalog, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Handling support requests: legitimate interest in communicating with users or performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(f) or (b) GDPR.

5. Registration and sign-in

Email sign-in: To confirm your address, we send you an email with a confirmation link.

Sign in with Apple: The provider is Apple. Apple transmits a user identifier and – depending on your choice – your real or an anonymized email address. For details, see Apple’s privacy policy.

Google sign-in: only if you choose this option. Google transmits a user identifier, your email address and the name stored in your Google account. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. For details, see Google’s privacy policy.

Securing your account: If you later link a guest account to Apple or Google, the same internal identifier is kept; your previous game data is preserved.

6. Hosting and data processing (Supabase)

We run our backend, database, authentication and server functions on the Supabase platform (provider: Supabase Inc., San Francisco, California, USA). The data is hosted in a data center within the European Union (Frankfurt am Main region, Germany; Amazon Web Services infrastructure).

We have concluded a data processing agreement with Supabase under Art. 28 GDPR. To the extent that access from the USA cannot be ruled out in the course of support or operations, it is safeguarded by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

7. Push notifications via Apple

Push notifications are delivered via the Apple Push Notification service (APNs). This involves processing a device token. The provider is Apple Inc., One Apple Park Way, Cupertino, California, USA, or Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland.

8. Purchases (Guess It Pro)

Paid features are handled exclusively through the Apple App Store. For the purchase itself, your contractual partner is Apple. Apple only tells us whether a valid purchase or an active subscription exists, plus a transaction identifier – but not your payment data. Subscriptions are managed and canceled in the App Store settings of your Apple account.

9. Recipients and international transfers

Recipients of your data are: Supabase (hosting/backend), Apple (sign-in, push, purchases) and – only if you sign in that way – Google (sign-in). We do not share data with other third parties unless we are legally required to.

Within the game, other players see your username, your avatar, your level and – in games with you, in friends lists and on leaderboards – game-related figures (e.g. points, XP, wins).

For transfers to the USA (Apple, Google, Supabase support where applicable), we rely on adequacy decisions (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) where the provider is certified, and otherwise on Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Retention

We store account, profile and game data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, this data is deleted immediately; ongoing games remain for your opponents without your personal details. Technical log data is kept only briefly and then deleted automatically. Reports are stored until the review is completed and for a reasonable period afterwards to prevent abuse. Submitted questions are kept after an account deletion, but without any link to your account. Data we must retain for tax or commercial law reasons (e.g. relating to purchases) is kept until the statutory periods expire and restricted from further processing in the meantime.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), as well as the right to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise these rights, contact guess-it@gmx.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

12. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account directly in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the associated data (profile, game records, friendships, device tokens).

13. No automated decision-making, no tracking

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. The app contains no third-party advertising, tracking or analytics services and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

14. Children

The app is not directed at children. Children under 13 may not use the app. If a user is between 13 and 15 years old, processing of their personal data requires the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, nor from minors under 16 without such consent. If we learn that this has happened anyway, we will promptly delete the account and the associated data. Parents and guardians can contact us at guess-it@gmx.de for this purpose.

15. Notice for users in the United States

We grant the rights described in section 11 to all users, regardless of where they live.

California (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (“sale” or “sharing” as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act), and we have not done so in the past twelve months. Sections 3, 4, 5 and 9 describe which data we process, where it comes from, what we use it for and who receives it; section 10 describes how long we keep it. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: The app does not track you across third-party apps or websites. Such signals therefore do not change how we process data.

Storage location: Your data is stored in the European Union (see section 6).
