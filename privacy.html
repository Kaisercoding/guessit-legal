2. Overview

Guess It is an estimation quiz that is mostly played in multiplayer mode. We process personal data for your account, for gameplay and for notifications. We limit processing to what is necessary to run the app. We do not sell data and use no tracking or analytics services.

The app is available for iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play). Where processing differs, we say so explicitly. The iOS version contains no ads. In the Android version, users without “Guess It Pro” occasionally see ads served by Google AdMob (see section 13); you decide in the consent dialog whether they are personalized.

3. Data we process

Account and sign-in data: If you register by email, we store your email address. With “Sign in with Apple” or Google sign-in, we receive a unique user identifier and – depending on what you choose with the provider – your email address (with Apple, possibly an anonymized relay address). Every account is assigned an internal identifier (UUID).

Guest account: If you play without registering (“Play right away”), we create an anonymous account without an email address. It is automatically given a name (e.g. “Guest-1042”).

Profile data: your chosen username and avatar (selected from predefined images), as well as the app language and your measurement system (metric or US). We take both from your device settings or from your choice in the app so that questions, units and notifications appear in your language. We do not collect your location.

Game data: ongoing and finished games, chosen categories, submitted guesses, round results, scores, experience points (XP), level, achievements, statistics and the times of your moves.

Friends data: your friends list as well as friend requests you sent and received.

Push notifications: a device token – issued by Apple on iOS and by Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) on Android – used to notify you when it is your turn or when you receive a friend request.

Purchase and subscription status: whether and which variant of “Guess It Pro” (monthly, yearly, one-time) is active, on which platform it was purchased, and a transaction identifier (a purchase token for Google Play). Payments are processed exclusively by Apple or Google Play; payment data (credit card etc.) never reaches us.

Advertising data (Android version only, only without “Guess It Pro”): When ads are loaded and shown, Google processes your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), your IP address, device and app information, interactions with the ads (e.g. impressions, clicks) and your consent choice. We ourselves only receive aggregated reports that do not identify you.

Reports: If you report a username or a quiz question, we store the report, the reason you selected, the identifier of the reported account or question and your identifier as the reporting person – for review by moderation and to prevent abuse.

Submitted questions: If you suggest a quiz question via “Submit a question”, we store the question text, answer, unit, category, source, the app language and your identifier.

Support communication: If you contact us, we process your message and contact details to handle your request.

Technical log data: When our servers are accessed, technical data is generated (including IP address, time, requested function, error and status messages, app and device metadata). It is used for secure operation, troubleshooting and defense against attacks, and is stored only briefly.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing your account, profile and gameplay (including multiplayer, friends, leaderboards, achievements, statistics): performance of the user agreement, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Push notifications: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (iOS or Android system prompt). You can withdraw it at any time in your device’s system settings or in the app.
  • Handling purchases and managing Pro status: performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and compliance with tax and commercial law obligations, Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.
  • Personalized ads and storing or accessing information on your device for advertising purposes (Android version only): your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG. Non-personalized ads: legitimate interest in financing the free basic version, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Handling reports, moderation, abuse prevention, IT security and log data: legitimate interest in a secure, functional and rule-compliant service, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Reviewing submitted questions and adding them to the question catalog: legitimate interest in a well-maintained, growing question catalog, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Handling support requests: legitimate interest in communicating with users or performance of contract, Art. 6(1)(f) or (b) GDPR.

5. Registration and sign-in

Email sign-in: To confirm your address, we send you an email with a confirmation link.

Sign in with Apple: The provider is Apple. Apple transmits a user identifier and – depending on your choice – your real or an anonymized email address. For details, see Apple’s privacy policy.

Google sign-in: only if you choose this option. Google transmits a user identifier, your email address and the name stored in your Google account. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. For details, see Google’s privacy policy.

The Android version offers Google sign-in and email.

Securing your account: If you later link a guest account to Apple or Google, the same internal identifier is kept; your previous game data is preserved.

6. Hosting and data processing (Supabase)

We run our backend, database, authentication and server functions on the Supabase platform (provider: Supabase Inc., San Francisco, California, USA). The data is hosted in a data center within the European Union (Frankfurt am Main region, Germany; Amazon Web Services infrastructure).

We have concluded a data processing agreement with Supabase under Art. 28 GDPR. To the extent that access from the USA cannot be ruled out in the course of support or operations, it is safeguarded by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

Images for questions: Photos that accompany some questions are loaded by the App exclusively from our own storage at Supabase (Supabase Storage); Supabase delivers them as our processor, possibly via a content delivery network of its sub-processors. The App does not connect to the original image sources (e.g. Wikimedia Commons, Flickr) or to any other third parties for this; only the technically necessary connection data (including IP address and time) is processed. Only if you tap a link to the license or the source in the image credit does the respective page open in your browser; the privacy policy of its provider applies there.

7. Push notifications via Apple and Google

On iOS, push notifications are delivered via the Apple Push Notification service (APNs). The provider is Apple Inc., One Apple Park Way, Cupertino, California, USA, or Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland.

On Android, they are delivered via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. We use Firebase solely for message delivery, not for any analytics features.

In both cases a device token is processed; the content of the notification (e.g. “Your turn”) passes through the respective service.

8. Purchases (Guess It Pro)

Paid features are handled exclusively through the Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). For the purchase itself, your contractual partner is Apple or Google. We are only told whether a valid purchase or an active subscription exists, plus a transaction identifier or purchase token – but not your payment data. To verify a Google Play purchase, our servers query the Google Play Developer API. Subscriptions are managed and canceled in the App Store settings of your Apple account or in Google Play under “Payments & subscriptions”.

The provider of Google Play is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

9. Recipients and international transfers

Recipients of your data are: Supabase (hosting/backend), Apple (sign-in, push and purchases on iOS) and Google (sign-in, if you choose it; on Android also push, purchases via Google Play and – without “Guess It Pro” – ads via AdMob). We do not share data with other third parties unless we are legally required to. With personalized ads, Google may pass data on to the advertising partners listed in its consent dialog – only with your consent.

Within the game, other players see your username, your avatar, your level and – in games with you, in friends lists and on leaderboards – game-related figures (e.g. points, XP, wins).

For transfers to the USA (Apple, Google, Supabase support where applicable), we rely on adequacy decisions (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) where the provider is certified, and otherwise on Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Retention

We store account, profile and game data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, this data is deleted immediately; ongoing games remain for your opponents without your personal details. Technical log data is kept only briefly and then deleted automatically. Reports are stored until the review is completed and for a reasonable period afterwards to prevent abuse. Submitted questions are kept after an account deletion, but without any link to your account. Data we must retain for tax or commercial law reasons (e.g. relating to purchases) is kept until the statutory periods expire and restricted from further processing in the meantime.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), as well as the right to object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Art. 21). You can withdraw any consent you have given at any time with effect for the future.

To exercise these rights, contact guess-it@gmx.de.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for us is the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht, BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

12. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account directly in the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the associated data (profile, game records, friendships, device tokens). The advertising ID of your Android device is managed by Android, not by the app – you can reset or delete it in the system settings.

13. Ads (Android only), no automated decision-making

iOS version: It contains no third-party advertising, tracking or analytics services and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA).

Android version: Without “Guess It Pro”, we occasionally show ads (a full-screen ad after a finished game, never during a turn). The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (Google AdMob). Before the first ad – in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland – we ask you via Google’s consent dialog (User Messaging Platform) whether Google may personalize ads and store or access information on your device for this purpose. If you decline, Google shows only non-personalized or limited ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options. With “Guess It Pro”, ads are removed entirely. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads for details on how Google processes data for ads.

There is no automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. Profiling for advertising purposes is carried out solely by Google and only if you have consented to personalized ads.

14. Children

The app is not directed at children. Children under 13 may not use the app. If a user is between 13 and 15 years old, processing of their personal data requires the consent of a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13, nor from minors under 16 without such consent. If we learn that this has happened anyway, we will promptly delete the account and the associated data. Parents and guardians can contact us at guess-it@gmx.de for this purpose.

15. Notice for users in the United States

We grant the rights described in section 11 to all users, regardless of where they live.

California (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell personal information. In the iOS version, we also do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising (“sharing” as defined by the California Consumer Privacy Act). In the Android version, personalized ads via Google AdMob (section 13) may count as “sharing”; you can opt out at any time under Settings → Privacy options (“Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information”), after which Google shows only non-personalized ads. We use sensitive personal information only to provide the app (e.g. for sign-in). Sections 3, 4, 5 and 9 describe which data we process, where it comes from, what we use it for and who receives it; section 10 describes how long we keep it. You can request access, correction and deletion, and you may authorize an agent to do so on your behalf. Send requests to guess-it@gmx.de; to verify your identity, we match the request against your account. Exercising these rights will not result in any discrimination against you.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control: The iOS version does not track you across third-party apps or websites. In the Android version, your choice under Settings → Privacy options applies to ads.

Storage location: Your data is stored in the European Union (see section 6).
